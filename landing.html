FAQ

Straight answers

Is it free?

Yes. The profile builder, chances view, roadmap and live dates are free to use — nothing is locked behind a plan.

Does it predict my admission odds?

No — and deliberately so. It shows Reach / High-reach fit bands derived from your profile against each school's emphasis weights, and tells you the levers that would change them.

Where does my data live?

In your browser's local storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server; export or reset any time from Settings.

What is ScholarAI?

A chat mentor powered by Groq models with a study-abroad system prompt. It receives a small snapshot of your profile (grade, major, score, top targets) so its advice is personal — the API key stays on the server, never in your browser.

Are the deadlines accurate?

SAT/ACT dates ship with sources and a verification stamp (verified Oct 2, 2026), and milestones always carry a reminder to confirm on official sites before you act.
